      Our playbook

      Every market has its own playbook. Ours is sharpest in B2B software.

      • Long cycles: 3 to 9 month deals, tracked all the way to closed-won.
      • Buying committees: ads that reach all six decision-makers, not just whoever clicks.
      • CRM-first: results reported in HubSpot or Salesforce, not the ad platform.

      Channels that carry it: LinkedIn, Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, G2 and Capterra.

      One team, from ad to pipeline

      • Acquire: ads that reach the whole buying committee.
      • Convert: landing pages and offers built to turn clicks into booked meetings.
      • Attribute: every dollar traced from the ad to the deal in your CRM.
      • Scale: spend moves every week to whatever is producing pipeline.

      We report sales-qualified leads, cost per opportunity and pipeline in your CRM, not impressions, clicks or platform ROAS.

      Who we serve

      A good fit: B2B software at $5M+ ARR or Series B and beyond, selling into the US and Canada, with deals worth $10K+ a year, already spending on paid.
