2The insight

Nobody searches for health. They search for a day.

People don't want 3,000 words on cortisol. They want to know what to do at 6 a.m. tomorrow.

So we stopped publishing articles and started publishing routines: timed, step by step, and checked by experts. The format is the brand, and it's exactly what the searcher came for.
