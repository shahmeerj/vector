2The insight

Andromeda reads the ad, not the settings.

Meta's new retrieval engine looks at the image, the words and the landing page, then goes looking for the people they describe. Targeting didn't disappear. It moved into the creative.

So instead of one ad for "astrology fans", we write many ads, each for one specific kind of person, and let the algorithm find each of them.

3What we built

A creative engine, not a campaign.

Andromeda tires of ads fast and rewards fresh ones. So we built a weekly rhythm: 40 new concepts every Monday, all running broad, the winners scaled and the rest cut by Friday.
