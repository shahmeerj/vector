1The problem

Tamba builds world-class apps and websites. But every new client came through a referral, and referrals arrive when they feel like it.

New-client conversations per month, the year before Vector. Sixteen in total.

2The insight

Leads go cold in five minutes.

Answer a lead within five minutes and you're 21 times more likely to qualify them than after thirty. Most firms take 42 hours. A CEO in back-to-back calls can't win that race, so we gave him something that can.

Relative odds of qualifying a lead by first response time. Sources: Lead Response Management Study; Harvard Business Review, 2011.

4Why the ads kept getting better

Meta learns from deals, not forms.

Every qualified chat and every signed contract goes back to Meta through the Conversions API. The algorithm stops chasing cheap form fills and starts finding people who look like Tamba's clients.

We let lead costs drift up slightly. The cost of a qualified call fell 68%.
