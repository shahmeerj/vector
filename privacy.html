Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information Vector collects when you visit meetvector.co, fill in one of our forms or see our ads, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Vector is a B2B performance marketing team based in Karachi, Pakistan. We run paid acquisition, landing pages and attribution for B2B companies. In this policy, "Vector", "we" and "us" mean us, and "you" means anyone who visits our website or contacts us.

For anything about your data, email shahmeer@thecatalystact.com.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Booking a call or a free audit: your name, work email and company website.
  • Applying for a job: your full name, date of birth, current city, email, phone number, LinkedIn profile (optional), notice period, expected salary (optional) and your CV.
  • Emailing or messaging us: whatever you choose to include.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical details: your IP address, browser and device type, the approximate location worked out from your IP address (country, region, city), your time zone, and the date and time of your visit.
  • How you reached us: the page you arrived on, the site that referred you, and campaign tags in the link (such as UTM parameters, a Google click ID or a LinkedIn click ID).
  • Advertising and measurement tags: when we run ads, we may use tags or pixels from Google, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), LinkedIn and Microsoft. These can set cookies and tell the platform that you visited a page or filled in a form, so we can measure results and show relevant ads. See "Ads and cookies" below.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, schedule calls and prepare for them, including looking at your company's public website and advertising.
  • To review job applications and contact applicants.
  • To understand which pages, campaigns and channels bring people to us, and to improve them.
  • To run, measure and improve our ads, including showing ads to people who visited our site and excluding people who are already in touch with us.
  • To keep the site secure and filter out spam and automated submissions.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (for example, for advertising cookies where consent is required), on steps you ask us to take before entering a contract (such as booking a call or applying for a job), and on our legitimate interest in running and promoting our business.

Ads and cookies

Our own site does not use cookies to identify you. It keeps a small note in your browser's local storage of how you first arrived (the referring site and campaign tags), so that if you later fill in a form we know which campaign brought you. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Advertising platforms whose tags we use may set their own cookies and collect information under their own privacy policies. They may use it to measure ads and to show you ads on their platforms and elsewhere. We may also give these platforms a hashed (scrambled) version of an email address to measure results or to stop showing ads to existing contacts.

You can control ads from these platforms here:

  • Google ad settings
  • Meta ad preferences
  • LinkedIn ad settings
  • Microsoft ad settings
  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out (US) and Your Online Choices (EU and UK)

Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of advertising use of your information.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with services that help us run Vector, and only as needed:

  • Vercel, which hosts this website and processes form submissions.
  • Google (Google Workspace), where we store form submissions, job applications and CVs, and send email.
  • Advertising platforms (Google, Meta, LinkedIn, Microsoft), as described above.
  • Professional advisers or authorities, if required by law or to protect our rights.

These providers may store data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, we rely on the safeguards they provide, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and call requests: up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client.
  • Job applications and CVs: up to 12 months after the role is filled, so we can consider you for similar roles, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Client information: for as long as we work together and as long afterwards as we are legally required to keep business records.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it, or delete it;
  • stop or limit how we use it, including for advertising;
  • withdraw consent you have given.

To make a request, email shahmeer@thecatalystact.com. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK and unhappy with our response, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

We use secure connections (HTTPS), limit who can access submitted information and keep it with reputable providers. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send us sensitive information we haven't asked for.

Children

Our services are for businesses and working professionals. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. We'll post the new version here and change the date at the top.
